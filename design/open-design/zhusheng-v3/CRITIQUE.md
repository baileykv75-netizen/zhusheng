# Zhusheng V3 Visual Critique

## Anti-AI-slop

- Pass: no purple/blue gradient, glow, glassmorphism, emoji icon, or nested card wall.
- Pass: corporate red and water cyan are bound to product semantics.
- Watch: do not let every status become a colored badge; most status should remain text.

## Typography hierarchy

- Every screen must expose one dominant entry point through both scale and whitespace.
- Page titles and current tasks must not compete at equal weight.
- Body and metadata sizes are bounded at 14px and 11px respectively.

## Accessibility

- Interactive controls target at least 44px on mobile and 40px on desktop.
- Corporate red is never the sole signal for an error or authorization state.
- Disclosures use native details/summary behavior and retain keyboard access.
- Motion is limited to opacity and short transforms and respects reduced-motion settings.

## Human review points

- Official corporate logo and final VI red remain pending.
- The BIM sample is intentionally desensitized and must be replaced only with approved media.
- The desktop composition should be tested at real 100% browser zoom, not judged only from full-page screenshots.
